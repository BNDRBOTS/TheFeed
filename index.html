THE FEED

you look. it looks back.
now you can't stop.

this isn't b r a n d.
it's appetite with a login.
dopamine dressed like design.

scroll. click. repeat.
you think you're choosing.
you're not.

light. sound. touch.
all hacked for impulse.
every detail designed
to keep your thumb moving.

we map your desires.
a psychological wiretap
disguised as community.

there is no bottom.
the algorithm is hungry.
feed it your attention.

you are not the user.
you are the harvest.

the only rule—
keep them craving.
